Welcome to VOCA

Celebrating Odia culture in British Columbia

Vancouver Odia Cultural Association is a non-profit cultural association based in Surrey, BC, Canada. Our mission is to promote cultural awareness and unity within the community.

  • Festivals like Rath Yatra, Raja Parba and Durga Puja
  • A welcoming community for Odia families across BC
  • Odia language, music, dance and arts for the next generation

Why Join VOCA

Joining VOCA means becoming part of a vibrant, welcoming community that celebrates the beauty of Odia culture while embracing the diversity of Canada. Whether you were born in Odisha, have Odia heritage, or simply share an interest in Indian culture, VOCA offers something meaningful for everyone.

Preserve and Share Your Heritage

Keep Odia traditions, language, music, dance, and festivals alive for future generations. Help your children understand and appreciate where they come from — while growing up proud Canadians.

Connect with Community

Meet fellow Odias and families across British Columbia. VOCA provides a warm, inclusive space to form friendships, network, and support one another through cultural, social, and educational activities.

Celebrate Together

Take part in events such as Raja Parba, Rath Yatra, Durga Puja, and Utkal Divas — filled with music, dance, food, and storytelling that bring Odisha to life right here in Canada.

Collaborate and Contribute

VOCA partners with other cultural organizations and Canadian institutions to promote multicultural understanding and community development. Members can volunteer, lead initiatives, or contribute their skills to strengthen our collective impact.

Belong to a Purpose-Driven Family

At VOCA, we value mutual respect, inclusivity, and harmony. We celebrate both Indian and Canadian values — creating a bridge between cultures, generations, and communities.

A Warm Welcome for Newcomers

New to British Columbia? VOCA helps newly arrived Odias settle into Canadian life — connecting you with established community members for friendship, guidance, and support from day one.

Join VOCA today — and be part of a growing family that honors its roots while shaping a vibrant, shared future in Canada.
