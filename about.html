Vancouver Odia Cultural Association

About Us

Welcome to Vancouver Odias. This page will help you understand our background, mission, vision, and values. We aim to connect with people from the province of Odisha in India who are new to Canada, providing a community and a cultural anchor as they settle in.

Who we are

Our Story

The Vancouver Odia Cultural Association (VOCA) is a nonprofit organization dedicated to preserving, promoting, and celebrating the rich cultural heritage of Odisha, India, within the Canadian multicultural landscape. Based in British Columbia, VOCA brings together Odia families and individuals who share a common bond with their roots in Odisha while embracing the values of Canadian society.

Our mission is to ensure that future generations remain connected to their Odia identity through language, art, dance, literature, and traditions, while fostering pride, inclusivity, and cultural awareness. From organizing cultural festivals, community gatherings, and educational initiatives to collaborating with Canadian cultural hubs and other communities, VOCA is committed to building bridges of friendship and understanding.

What guides us

Our Values & Vision

Our Values

We believe in mutual respect, inclusivity, and cultural harmony. Guided by these values, we strive to create a welcoming space where Odia culture thrives alongside Canada's diverse multicultural mosaic.

Together, we celebrate our past, honor our traditions, and inspire future generations to carry forward the timeless spirit of Odisha—rooted in heritage, flourishing in Canada.

Our Vision

Vancouver Odias is more than just a non-profit; it's a place to keep our ancestry, culture, traditions, art, and language alive and pass them on to new generations.

We strive to be a thriving and inclusive Odia cultural hub in British Columbia that inspires pride in our art, heritage, connects generations, and builds bridges of friendship and understanding within the broader Canadian multicultural landscape.

What We Do

Vibrant Cultural Celebrations

We organize and celebrate major Odia and Indian festivals, ensuring that the traditions and customs are passed down and enjoyed by all. These include:

  • Raja Parba: The traditional Odia festival welcoming the monsoon and honouring the earth.
  • Rath Yatra: The majestic chariot festival, a spiritual and communal spectacle.
  • Durga Puja: Celebrating the goddess Durga with rituals, music, and community feasting.
  • Utkal Divas: A significant day commemorating the formation of the state of Odisha, filled with cultural performances and reflections on our heritage.
  • Diwali: The festival of lights, symbolizing the triumph of light over darkness.

Engaging Community Activities

Beyond festivals, we believe in holistic community engagement. Our activities are designed to cater to diverse interests and age groups:

  • Social Gatherings & Picnics: Informal get-togethers that help families across British Columbia connect.
  • Promoting Odia Arts & Literature: We actively encourage and showcase Odia artistic talents, including music, dance, literature, and traditional crafts, providing a platform for expression and appreciation.
  • Multicultural Collaboration: We partner with other cultural organizations and Canadian institutions to promote multicultural understanding and community development.
  • Community Support & Networking: VOCA serves as a valuable resource for newly arrived Odias, helping them settle into Canadian society and connecting them with established community members for support and networking opportunities.
